The Teacher

Mathify is primarily run by Tanmay Mogra, an IIT alumnus and a Mathematics Teacher having a teaching experience of 9+ years. He cleared the Joint Entrance Exam (JEE Advanced - touted to be one of the toughest undergraduate entrance exams in the world) in 2007, with an All India Rank of 328. He has a very strong hold of High School Maths Topics that are taught across the globe, which he learnt while preparing for the IITJEE, and from his own self-study and experience. He has taught 500+ students at prominent coaching institutes in India, and in private for students across countries (USA, UK, UAE, Singapore, India), and across curricula (JEE, IBDP, IGCSE, ICSE, ISC, CBSE). He is very passionate about both Maths and Teaching, and has developed a great knack for teaching students in an enjoyable way.
